# Milk Nest Public Website Wireframe Plan

## Scope

This wireframe plan covers only the public website for Milk Nest. It does not include backend features, admin screens, mobile app screens, login, dashboard, or farm operation forms.

## Website Type

Recommended first version: single-page public website.

The site should feel polished on mobile first, with desktop as an expanded layout. Most visitors are expected to use phones, so the mobile experience should prioritize clarity, fast scanning, and easy contact.

## Menu Items

- Home
- Services
- Why Us
- How It Helps
- Contact

Primary call to action:

- Contact Us

## Screen / Section Flow

1. Header
   - Milk Nest logo
   - Desktop menu links
   - Mobile menu icon
   - Contact button on desktop
   - Sticky behavior after scroll

2. Hero
   - Strong headline about smarter dairy farm operations
   - Short supporting paragraph
   - Primary button: Contact Us
   - Secondary button: View Services
   - Real dairy/farm visual or generated farm operations visual

3. Services
   - Cattle management
   - Milk production tracking
   - Sales and expense monitoring
   - Staff attendance
   - Health and vaccination records
   - Branch-wise farm monitoring
   - Reports and insights
   - Feed and resource tracking

4. Why Us
   - Less manual record keeping
   - Better daily visibility
   - Organized cattle information
   - Useful for single or multi-branch farms
   - Helps monitor money flow
   - Designed for practical dairy farm users

5. How It Helps
   - Record daily farm activity
   - Monitor production, sales, and expenses
   - Improve decisions with reports

6. Contact
   - Name
   - Phone
   - Email
   - Farm or business name
   - Message
   - Contact details block

7. Footer
   - Short Milk Nest description
   - Navigation links
   - Contact links
   - Social placeholders

## Mobile UX Priorities

- Put the hero visual before or very close to the hero message.
- Keep the headline short and readable.
- Use full-width buttons with at least 44px height.
- Use one-column service cards.
- Keep form fields large and easy to tap.
- Keep animations subtle and fast.
- Avoid text-heavy sections.
- Keep contact access visible from the top and bottom of the page.

## Animation Direction

- Header gently appears and becomes compact on scroll.
- Hero text fades upward on load.
- Hero image uses very subtle parallax or floating motion.
- Service cards reveal one by one as the user scrolls.
- Why Us points slide in from the side on desktop and fade upward on mobile.
- Contact form fades in after the section heading.
- Respect reduced motion settings for users who disable animation.

## Responsive Breakpoints

- Mobile: up to 560px
- Tablet: 561px to 900px
- Desktop: above 900px
